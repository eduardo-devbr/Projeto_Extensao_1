\begin{resumo}[Abstract]			% Título do Resumo (Abstract = Resumo em inglês)
\begin{otherlanguage*}{english}		% Língua do texto
This work presents a project developed to the subject of Integrated Extension Project (final course project) for the higher education course Analysis and System Development of the Federal Institution of Education, Science and Technology of São Paulo. The proposal is based on the development of a web application to support the management of 'Sebo do Cesar', a store specialized at selling used books. This project was conceived on knowledge obtained throughout the course and structured on software engineering practices, including requirements lifting, business rules, data modeling, architeture defining of the solution and the choosing of the used technologies of development. Besides attending to the partner needs, the project allows practical application of the academic bases in a real scenario, reforcing integration beetwen education, technology and entension.
\vspace{\onelineskip}
\noindent

\textbf{Keywords}: Management; Books; Web Systems; Software Development.
\end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
